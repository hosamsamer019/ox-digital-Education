## 1. Design Overview & Screen Hierarchy

The **Cashbox Management Workspace** provides a cohesive, RTL-first, tabular-numeric interface designed to integrate seamlessly with the Phase 2 App Shell and financial design tokens.

### Screen Identifier Map:
- **`CBM-OVR-01`:** Institutional Cashbox Directory & Liquidity Overview
- **`CBM-DET-01`:** Cashbox Detail & Transaction Ledger
- **`CBM-TRF-01`:** Internal Cashbox Transfers Workspace (5-Stage Lifecycle)
- **`CBM-REC-01`:** Daily Reconciliation & Shift Count Ledger
- **`CBM-DISC-01`:** Discrepancy Investigation Dossier (`UP-705` Neutral Status)
- **`CBM-EXP-01`:** Expense Voucher Management & Drafting Drawer
- **`CBM-COLL-01`:** Cash Intake Ingestion & Receipt Verification Modal
- **`CBM-AUD-01`:** Forensic Audit Trail & Document Vault Modal
