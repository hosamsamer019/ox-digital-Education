## 1. Overview of Primary Manager Screen Architecture

The Primary Manager suite consists of seven core operational modules accessible from the unified application shell:
1. **Screen A: Main Executive Dashboard (`PM-DASH-01`)** — High-level liquidity, collections, dues, and approvals.
2. **Screen B: Branch Management & Multi-Campus Register (`PM-BR-01`)** — Physical and digital campus oversight without a Branch Manager role (`OD-701`).
3. **Screen C: Student Financial Accounts & Ledger (`PM-STU-01`)** — Four-tier contract vs due vs collected vs earned separation.
4. **Screen D: Multi-Cashbox Governance & Daily Reconciliation (`CB-MGT-01`)** — Tills, vaults, transfers, and fail-closed discrepancy review (`UP-705`).
5. **Screen E: Collections Monitoring & Expense Authorizations (`PM-COLL-01` & `PM-EXP-01`)** — Frontline receipt logs and Maker-Checker voucher checks.
6. **Screen F: Teacher Educational Evidence & Settlement Oversight (`PM-TCH-01`)** — Delivered lesson evidence under fail-closed compensation rules (`UP-704`).
7. **Screen G: Audited Financial Reports Center (`PM-RPT-01`)** — Actual historical reporting without speculative forecasting or tax modules.
