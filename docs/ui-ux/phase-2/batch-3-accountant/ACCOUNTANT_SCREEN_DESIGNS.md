## 1. Overview of Accountant Screen Architecture

The Accountant Financial Operations Workspace consists of nine operational modules accessible from the unified application shell:
1. **Screen ACC-01: Accountant Operational Dashboard** — Real-time till balances, today's collections, pending Maker submissions, quick collection desk.
2. **Screen ACC-02: Student Financial Accounts & Ledger** — Four-tier student sub-ledgers, payment schedules, and verified receipt histories.
3. **Screen ACC-03: Collection Workspace & Desk** — High-speed receipt entry with explicit allocation targeting to scheduled payments.
4. **Screen ACC-04: Receipt Management & Audit** — Search, verification, reprinting, and append-only compensating reversals (no destructive deletion).
5. **Screen ACC-05: Expense Preparation & Voucher Submission** — Voucher drafting, cost center assignment, flexible supporting document attachments.
6. **Screen ACC-06: Cashbox Operations & Transfer Desk** — Authorized campus tills (`CB-01`, `CB-02`), transfer requests, and cash movement logs.
7. **Screen ACC-07: Daily Reconciliation & Blind Count** — End-of-day physical count entry, variance calculation, and fail-closed `UP-705` locks.
8. **Screen ACC-08: Teacher Financial Evidence & Attendance Logs** — Delivered lessons, certified attendance evidence, holding dues pending `UP-704`.
9. **Screen ACC-09: Operational Reports Center** — Preliminary factual reports without forecasting or tax functionality.
